Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 9, 2026 • Version 1.0.3

This Privacy Policy describes how Winlay ("we", "us", or "our") protects your privacy and handles data when you use the Winlay mobile application.

1. Core Privacy Guarantee

Winlay is built with a strict privacy-first architecture. We do NOT collect personal identification information, track your GPS location, access private device files, or sell data to advertisers. No user account, registration, or sign-in is required to use the application.

2. Local Device Preferences

Your personal app configuration (such as dark or AMOLED theme mode, custom UI color accent, and mobile or console interface layout) is stored exclusively within your device's private local sandbox using Android DataStore. This data never leaves your device.

3. Online Services & Catalog Synchronization

Winlay connects to Google Firebase via encrypted HTTPS/TLS solely to provide up-to-date operating system catalog entries and news announcements. We do not transmit device identifiers (IMEI/Android ID) or usage telemetry profiles.

4. Companion Apps & Package Visibility

On handheld console mode, Winlay queries your device locally to detect whether companion emulators (such as Termux, Redbox PC Emulator, or Winlator) are installed so you can launch them with one tap. This check happens entirely offline on the client device without sending installed app lists anywhere.

5. Official Updates

Winlay distributes official application updates through the Google Play Store. When you check for updates, Winlay directs you to the official Google Play Store listing. The app does not download or silently install APK updates in the background.

6. External Links & Mirrors

External links (such as official tutorial channels, developer profiles, community Discord servers, and official software distributor mirrors) are opened using your default external web browser or apps. Your interactions with third-party sites are governed by their respective privacy terms.

7. Children's Privacy

Winlay is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security & Data Retention

Because all preferences and cache reside strictly in the local application sandbox, uninstalling the app or clearing application storage through Android Settings immediately removes all stored configuration data.
